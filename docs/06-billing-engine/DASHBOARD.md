# Future Dashboard — Billing Engine

Owner/manager money screen, fed entirely by the sign-in/out register and rate card.

- **Live today:** children in building × their plan = revenue accruing right now; hourly children's running clocks
- **Unbilled:** attended hours not yet invoiced (should trend to zero every Friday)
- **Invoices:** issued / paid / overdue, with aging bands (0–3 / 4–10 / 11–21 / 21+ days) and next automatic reminder date
- **Auto-notifications feed:** naptime, arrival, pickup, invoice, reminder messages sent today (with delivery/read status)
- **Plan mix:** revenue by plan (hourly / half-day / full-day / monthly) — pricing strategy signal
- **Late-pickup fees:** auto-flagged from sign-out times vs plan end times
- **Forecast:** this month's projected revenue from enrollment × plans vs actual collections
