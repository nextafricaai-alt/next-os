# Daily Recording Procedure

> The rule: record at the MOMENT of capture, on your device, in the child's daily log. Never batch at day end — memory is not a system.

## Moments of capture
| When | What you record | Where | Time budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Child arrives | Time, who dropped, mood, parent notes | Daily log — Arrival | 20 sec |
| Each meal ends | What served, how much eaten (kitchen pre-confirms allergy check) | Daily log — Meals | 20 sec |
| Child wakes from nap | Sleep window, quality | Daily log — Naps | 15 sec |
| Each diaper change / toilet visit | Status, notes | Daily log — Toileting | 10 sec |
| During free play (once daily) | One specific moment worth telling the parent | Daily log — Learning | 30 sec |
| When it happens | Faith/character moment, health observation | Daily log — relevant section | 30 sec |
| Child leaves | Time, pickup person VERIFIED against list | Daily log — Departure | 20 sec |

## Deadlines
- All logs complete by **4:00pm** (manager spot-checks at 4:15)
- Parent updates auto-drafted from logs by **5:00pm**, teacher reviews and sends by **5:30pm**

## Quality standard
- Specific beats general. "Built a 6-block tower unaided" not "played well."
- If you didn't see it, don't write it. If something's wrong, flag it — never smooth it over.
- Incidents NEVER go only in the daily log — they trigger the incident procedure (see safety-protocols.md).

## Device rules
- Center devices only; no personal photos of children on personal phones.
- Photos: only of children with media consent ☐ checked in master record.
