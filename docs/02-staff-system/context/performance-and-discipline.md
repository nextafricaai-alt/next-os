# Staff Performance & Discipline

> High turnover kills childcare quality. We manage performance proactively to retain great staff, and we use structured discipline to protect the children from poor performers.

## 1. The Bi-Weekly Check-in
Branch Managers must hold a 10-minute check-in with every staff member every two weeks.
- **Goal:** Pulse check on wellbeing, ask if they need support with any specific children, and provide micro-feedback on their interactions.
- These check-ins are logged in the Staff Record.

## 2. Formal Disciplinary Procedure
If a staff member breaches a core policy (e.g., leaving children unattended, using a mobile phone in a classroom, inappropriate language):
- **Strike 1:** Verbal warning (documented in OS).
- **Strike 2:** Written warning + mandatory retraining.
- **Strike 3:** Termination.
*Note: Severe safeguarding breaches skip the strike system and result in immediate termination and reporting to local authorities.*

## 3. The Exit Interview
Whenever a staff member resigns, the Global Director (not the Branch Manager) conducts a 15-minute exit interview to uncover systemic issues.
- **Key Questions:** "Did you feel supported by the manager?", "Was the workload fair?", "What would have convinced you to stay?"
- This data feeds directly into the Owner's Quality Scorecard to track staff retention health.
