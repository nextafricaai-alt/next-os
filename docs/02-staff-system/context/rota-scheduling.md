# Staff Rota & Scheduling

> Ratios are not a policy problem — they are a rostering problem. This document defines who covers every hour of every day, how breaks are managed without breaking ratios, and how sick-day gaps are filled before the first child arrives.

## 1. Minimum Staffing Table

| Room | Age Band | Max Children | Min Staff | Ratio |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Infants | 0–18 months | 6 | 2 | 1:3 |
| Toddlers | 18m–3 years | 10 | 2 | 1:5 |
| Preschool | 3–5 years | 16 | 2 | 1:8 |
| School-Age | 5–8 years | 20 | 2 | 1:10 |

> **Golden Rule:** Ratios must be maintained at all times — including during staff breaks. If a room cannot be covered, the room closes to new arrivals until staffing is restored.

---

## 2. Weekly Rota Template

The Branch Manager publishes the rota **every Friday by 4 PM** for the following week. Staff acknowledge their shifts by end-of-day Saturday.

| Shift | Time | Role Required |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Opening | 07:00 – 15:00 | Lead Educator + 1 Assistant |
| Mid | 09:00 – 17:00 | 1 Educator (overlap covers lunch break) |
| Closing | 11:00 – 19:00 | 1 Educator + 1 Assistant |
| Float | On-Call | 1 On-Call per branch per day |

> Staff may not be scheduled for more than 5 consecutive days or more than 48 hours in any 7-day period.

---

## 3. Break Coverage Rules

Breaks may not be taken simultaneously by two staff from the same room.

- **30-minute breaks:** Covered by the Float or Mid-shift educator stepping in.
- **Lunch (1 hour):** Staggered — Room A staff eat at 12:00, Room B at 13:00. The Float covers whichever room is short.
- **Emergency toilet/absence:** Any staff member may call "cover needed" and the Float responds within 3 minutes. If no Float is available, the Branch Manager covers personally.

---

## 4. On-Call Cover Chain

Every branch must maintain a minimum **3-person on-call pool** at all times.

1. **First call:** The designated Float for that day.
2. **Second call:** Part-time staff member on standby (rostered to on-call 2× per month max).
3. **Third call:** Branch Manager covers the floor while calling an agency/substitute teacher.

> If all 3 fail, the Branch Manager calls the Global Director and the center operates at reduced capacity (one room closes) until cover arrives.

---

## 5. Sick-Day Protocol

When a staff member calls in sick **before 7:00 AM:**
1. Branch Manager checks the `onCallPool` in the OS immediately.
2. First available on-call is contacted and must confirm availability within 15 minutes.
3. If no cover by 07:30, the room's parent group is notified that capacity is reduced for the day (no new walk-ins accepted).
4. The sick day is logged by the Branch Manager in the Staff Record the same day.

---

## 6. Rota Change Control

- No swap may reduce a room below minimum ratio.
- All swaps must be logged in the OS by the initiating staff member and approved by the Branch Manager.
- Retroactive swaps (logged after the shift) are not permitted and are treated as an unexcused absence for the original staff member.

---

## 7. Holiday & Peak Period Planning

- School holiday periods require an **extended rota** published **3 weeks in advance** (not 1 week).
- Expected enrollment surge weeks (September intake, January intake) require a minimum of 1 additional float on standby each day.
- The Global Director must approve any rota that requires more than 2 agency staff in a single week.
