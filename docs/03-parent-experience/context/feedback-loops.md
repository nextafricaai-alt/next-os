# Continuous Feedback Instruments

> We do not wait for parents to complain to fix an issue. We proactively seek feedback to continuously iterate the Armani OS experience.

## 1. The Weekly Pulse (CSAT)
Parents are prompted once a week within the Parent App to rate their experience out of 5 stars and leave a brief comment.
- **Goal:** Catch micro-frustrations (e.g., "The drop-off line was slow on Tuesday") before they become macro-churn events.
- **Routing:** Any rating of 3 stars or below immediately flags the Branch Manager for a mandatory follow-up phone call within 24 hours.

## 2. The Quarterly Deep Dive
A comprehensive survey sent every 3 months evaluating:
- Educational Curriculum satisfaction.
- Nutritional menu feedback.
- Cleanliness and safety perceptions.
- Staff interactions.

## 3. Incident Follow-Ups
If a child has a documented incident (e.g., a scraped knee or a behavioral challenge), the Branch Manager must personally call the parents 48 hours later to check on the child's well-being and gather feedback on how the staff handled the event.
