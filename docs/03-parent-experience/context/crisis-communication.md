# Crisis Communication Playbook

> In a crisis, silence breeds panic. We must communicate rapidly, transparently, and with authority to maintain parent trust.

## 1. The 60-Minute Rule
In the event of a critical incident (e.g., severe child injury requiring an ambulance, building fire, lockdown, or public health outbreak):
- The Branch Manager must inform the Global Director within 5 minutes.
- The Global Director must send a unified, factual message to all affected parents within 60 minutes.
- **Do not wait for perfect information.** If facts are still developing, communicate that: "We are currently handling [Situation] at the center. All children are safe. We will provide a full update by [Time]."

## 2. Channel Discipline
- **Do not use WhatsApp groups for crisis communication.** WhatsApp breeds uncontrolled chatter and speculation.
- All crisis communications must be sent as 1-way global broadcast messages through the Armani OS Parent App or via direct SMS/Email from the central office.
- Direct phone calls must be made to the parents of any directly involved children BEFORE the global broadcast goes out.

## 3. Media Policy
Branch Managers and staff are strictly forbidden from speaking to journalists or posting about center incidents on personal social media.
- All media inquiries must be met with: "Please contact our Global Director for an official statement."
- The Global Director is the sole authorized spokesperson for the Armani brand.
