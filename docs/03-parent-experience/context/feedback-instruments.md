# Feedback Survey & Exit Interview Instruments

> These are the actual instruments — not descriptions of instruments. Copy these exactly when running the Termly Parent Survey or a Staff Exit Interview.

---

## INSTRUMENT 1 — Termly Parent Survey (5 Questions)

> Sent via the Parent App at the end of every school term. Takes under 3 minutes to complete. Results are compiled by the Branch Manager and reviewed with the Global Director within 7 days of term end.

**Q4. How likely are you to recommend Armani to another family?**

**Q5. What is the ONE thing we could do to make next term even better for your family?**
_____________________________________________________________
_____________________________________________________________

---

> **Scoring:** Questions 1–4 are scored 5→1 (Very Satisfied/Likely = 5). Max score = 20 per family. Branch average target: **≥ 17/20** (85%). Any branch scoring below 15 triggers a mandatory Branch Manager action plan reviewed with the Global Director within 14 days.

> **Low-score follow-up:** Any individual response with Q1 or Q4 rated "Dissatisfied" or below triggers a personal phone call from the Branch Manager within 48 hours.
