# Marketing & Enrollment Growth Engine

> Occupancy percentage is the single biggest lever for profitability. The Growth Engine ensures our waitlist is always full and our pipeline is active.

## 1. The Referral Reward Program
Word of mouth from existing parents is our highest-converting acquisition channel.
- **The Offer:** If an existing parent refers a family that enrolls and stays for 1 full month, the referring parent receives a 10% discount on their next month's invoice.
- **Execution:** Automated prompt via the Parent App after a child reaches their 90-day enrollment milestone (assuming high satisfaction).

## 2. Community Partnerships
Instead of broad marketing, target where parents of young children already gather.
- **Local Primary Schools:** Establish a "feeder" relationship. We provide after-school care pickups from 3 targeted primary schools within a 3km radius.
- **Local Churches/Mosques:** Offer a Sunday/Friday creche service or sponsor their family events to build brand awareness.
- **Corporate HR:** Pitch to local mid-sized businesses as their "preferred childcare provider" for returning mothers, offering a waived registration fee.

## 3. The Waitlist Nurture Cadence
A waitlist is useless if parents enroll elsewhere before we have a spot.
- **Week 1:** Welcome email + digital copy of our "First 1000 Days" curriculum guide.
- **Week 4:** SMS check-in from the Branch Manager ("Hi! Still keeping your spot warm on the waitlist. How is [Child's Name] doing?").
- **Week 8:** Invite the family to our next Open Day or a free weekend play-session to keep them engaged with the facility.

## 4. The Open Day Run Sheet
Held once per quarter on a Saturday morning.
- 09:30 AM: Doors open, free play in the main yard.
- 10:00 AM: Director's Welcome & Curriculum Presentation.
- 10:30 AM: Facility Tour (led by Lead Teachers).
- 11:00 AM: On-the-spot registration with a "Sign Today" incentive (e.g., free uniform set).
