# Center Launch Playbook

> The blueprint for scaling Armani OS and opening new centers. A center is not ready to open until all phases are complete.

## Phase 1: Site & Foundation (Months -4 to -3)
- **Site Selection:** Minimum 200 sqm interior, secure outdoor play area, residential zoning approval.
- **Financial Model:** Breakeven analysis calculated, minimum 12-month runway secured in escrow.
- **Lease Execution:** Minimum 5-year lease signed with a break clause at year 2.

## Phase 2: Compliance & Buildout (Months -3 to -2)
- **Licensing:** Submit application to local educational authorities.
- **Fire & Safety:** Install alarms, extinguishers, secure local fire department certificate.
- **Buildout:** Child-safe flooring, low sinks, secure entry systems installed.
- **IT Setup:** High-speed internet, operations wall tablets mounted, backup power (UPS) installed.

## Phase 3: Hiring & Training (Months -2 to -1)
- **Branch Manager Hire:** Must be hired at least 8 weeks prior to launch.
- **Manager Immersion:** Manager spends 4 weeks shadowing at the flagship/Center #1 to learn Armani OS.
- **Staff Recruitment:** Hire educators for all age groups based on starting ratio projections.
- **Staff Training Week:** Full team undergoes curriculum, safeguarding, and Armani OS digital training (1 week prior to opening).

## Phase 4: Marketing & Pre-Enrollment (Month -1)
- **Waitlist Activation:** Contact all waitlisted families in the new geographic area.
- **Community Partnerships:** Outreach to 3 local primary schools and 2 local churches.
- **Open House Event:** Host community tour 2 weeks before launch.
- **Target:** Secure 30% capacity paid enrollment before opening day.

## Phase 5: The First 90 Days (Post-Launch)
- **Week 1-2 (Hypercare):** Global Director on-site 3 days a week. Focus on routing errors, parent app adoption, and staff ratio adherence.
- **Month 1 Review:** Audit all digital logs vs paper fallbacks.
- **Month 2 Review:** First parent satisfaction pulse survey (5 questions).
- **Month 3 Review:** Evaluate Branch Manager performance, transition to standard monthly reporting.
