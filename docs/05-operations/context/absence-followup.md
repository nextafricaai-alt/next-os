# Absence Management & Follow-up

> An unannounced absence is a breakdown in care. We track absence not just for billing, but for safeguarding and community health.

## 1. The 9:30 AM Rule
By 9:30 AM, daily attendance must be fully logged in Armani OS. 
Any child scheduled to attend who is not signed in, and whose parents have not notified the center via the Parent App, must receive a **Safeguarding Call** from the Branch Manager.
- **Why:** To ensure the child is safe (e.g., they weren't forgotten in a hot car, or the family isn't experiencing an emergency).

## 2. Illness Tracking & Outbreak Detection
When a child is absent due to illness, the specific symptom (e.g., fever, rash, vomiting) must be logged in their profile.
- If **three or more children** in the same room report the same contagious symptom within 48 hours, the Branch Manager must trigger an outbreak notification to all parents in that room and escalate cleaning protocols.

## 3. Return to Care
Following a communicable illness (e.g., Hand, Foot, and Mouth disease, severe conjunctivitis), the child may not return without a doctor's clearance note or having been symptom-free without medication for 24 hours. The Operations Tablet will flag the child with a "Return Check Required" banner upon their next check-in.
