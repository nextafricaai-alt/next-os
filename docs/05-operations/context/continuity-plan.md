# Business Continuity & Fallback Systems

> Armani OS is a digital-first system, but childcare is a physical-first reality. The center must continue to operate safely even if the power fails, the internet drops, or a device breaks.

## 1. The Paper Fallback Pack
Every classroom must maintain a "Continuity Clipboard" hanging next to the door. This clipboard must contain:
- Printed emergency contact lists for every enrolled child (updated monthly).
- Printed daily log sheets (Sign-in/out, feeding, napping).
- Printed allergy matrix.

If the tablets go down, staff immediately switch to the clipboard.

## 2. Catch-up Data Entry
When systems are restored (power/internet returns):
- The Branch Manager must ensure that all paper logs are manually entered into Armani OS before the end of the shift.
- No staff member leaves until the digital ledger perfectly matches the paper fallback sheet.

## 3. Device Charging Rota
Dead tablets are an operational failure. 
- All tablets must be docked in the charging station every night at 6:00 PM.
- The morning opening manager must verify that all devices are at 100% battery before 7:00 AM.
- Tablets should be set to aggressive power-saving modes (screen timeout at 30 seconds) to ensure they survive a 10-hour shift without charging.
