## ⚠️ Allergy cross-check procedure (BLOCKING — every meal service)
1. Kitchen prints/opens today's allergy list (from 01 health files) before cooking.
2. Any child with an allergen in today's menu gets a named, covered alternative plate.
3. Room teacher confirms the check ☐ in the daily log before serving.
4. New enrollment or updated allergy = kitchen briefed same day, list reprinted.
