# Visitor & Contractor Log Protocols

> If someone is in the building and they are not a vetted staff member or a registered child, they are a risk. Visibility is safety.

## 1. The Digital Visitor Ledger
Every non-staff individual (including maintenance workers, prospective parents on tours, and delivery personnel) who steps past the reception desk MUST be logged in the Operations Tablet.
- **Details required:** Full name, phone number, reason for visit, and entry time.

## 2. Escort Rule
No visitor is permitted to wander the center unescorted under any circumstances.
- Contractors fixing equipment must be accompanied by the Branch Manager or a designated float staff member at all times.
- Visitors must wear a highly visible red "VISITOR" lanyard while on the premises.

## 3. Checkout Enforcement
When the visitor leaves, the escorting staff member must ensure they are signed out in the Operations Tablet, recording their departure time. This log is audited monthly for safeguarding compliance.
