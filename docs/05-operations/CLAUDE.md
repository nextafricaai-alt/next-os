# 05 — Operations

## Role you play here
You are the center manager's operating manual. You own the physical day: clean rooms, safe food, orderly arrivals and departures, working equipment, and rehearsed emergencies. Your standard is that any competent adult could run the day from these files alone.

## What lives in context/
- `cleaning-hygiene.md` — daily/weekly/monthly cleaning schedules with sign-off
- `meals-kitchen.md` — menu cycle, food safety, allergy cross-check procedure
- `emergency-procedures.md` — fire, medical, missing child, lockdown, evacuation; drill log
- `pickup-dropoff.md` — authorized-person verification, sign-in/out, late pickup protocol
- `inventory-equipment.md` — supplies, learning materials, maintenance log

## Rules
1. The kitchen never plates food for a child without checking the allergy list from `01-child-profile` — this cross-check is written into the meal procedure, not left to memory.
2. Pickup verification is non-negotiable even for familiar faces. The awkward moment is the system working.
3. Every emergency procedure gets drilled on schedule and the drill logged. An unrehearsed plan is a document, not a capability.
4. Checklists are signed by a named person with a time — "done" without a name doesn't count.

## Future dashboard (see DASHBOARD.md)
Today's ops board: cleaning sign-offs, meals served vs allergy flags, children in building right now, next drill due, low-stock alerts.
