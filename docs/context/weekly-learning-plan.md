# Weekly Learning Plan & Curriculum

> Armani OS tracks milestones, but this document defines how those milestones are achieved. The Curriculum is the input; the Milestones are the output.

## 1. The Core Weekly Structure
Each week across all Armani centers follows a unified "Theme of the Week" (e.g., "Community Helpers", "Under the Sea", "Harvest & Gratitude").
- **Mondays:** Introduction of the weekly theme and new vocabulary.
- **Tuesdays:** Hands-on craft or sensory activity related to the theme.
- **Wednesdays:** Physical development and outdoor theme exploration.
- **Thursdays:** Cognitive/Math concepts tied to the theme (counting shells, sorting shapes).
- **Fridays:** Celebration, recap, and "Show & Tell".

## 2. The Faith Curriculum Rotation
Faith is a core pillar of the Armani brand identity. 
- **Monthly Memory Verse:** One short scripture memorized over 4 weeks using hand motions.
- **Weekly Bible Story:** Taught during Morning Circle (e.g., Noah's Ark, David & Goliath).
- **Daily Worship:** 10 minutes of age-appropriate worship songs before the morning lesson.

## 3. Linkage to Milestones
Educators are required to design activities that trigger observable milestones:
- *Fine Motor:* Threading beads (triggers observation for "Pincer Grasp").
- *Social-Emotional:* Group building blocks (triggers observation for "Sharing and Turn-taking").

## 4. Parent Visibility
The Weekly Learning Plan must be published to the `ParentApp` every Sunday at 5:00 PM so parents can continue the learning conversations at home.
