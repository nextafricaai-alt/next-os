# Child Transitions & Graduation

> A child's move to a new room or a new school is not an administrative event — it is a milestone for the family and a marketing asset for Armani. Every transition must be warm, documented, and celebrated.

---

## 1. Room-to-Room Transitions (Toddler → Preschool → School-Age)

### Readiness Assessment
A child is ready to transition when they meet **at least 80%** of the developmental milestones for their current age-band, as recorded in `milestones-development.md`. Readiness is assessed by:
- The Lead Educator of the child's current room.
- Confirmed by the Branch Manager.
- Communicated to parents **at least 4 weeks before** the transition date.

### Transition Period
| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| Week 1 | Child visits the new room for 1 hour per day (with familiar educator present). |
| Week 2 | Child spends a half-day in new room. Lead Educator of new room introduced to family. |
| Week 3 | Full-day trial. Parents receive a written "Transition Note" with observations. |
| Week 4 | Full transition. Child's complete profile (health, allergy, milestone, daily-log data) is transferred to new room's Lead Educator. |

### Data Handoff Checklist
Before transition is marked complete in the OS, the following must be confirmed:
- [ ] Child Master Record reviewed and updated.
- [ ] Allergies & health record confirmed with new Lead Educator and signed off.
- [ ] Milestone progress notes transferred.
- [ ] Parent notified in writing (message sent via Parent App).
- [ ] Emergency contact details re-verified with parent.

## 2. Graduation (Departing to Primary School)

### Graduation Portfolio
Every child leaving Armani for primary school receives a **Graduation Portfolio** — a printed and digital keepsake prepared by their Lead Educator, containing:
1. A personal cover letter from the Branch Manager addressed to the child.
2. Key developmental milestones achieved (from `milestones-development.md`).
3. Faith character highlights (memory verses learned, Bible stories covered).
4. A selection of photos from the Gallery (3–5 best images from the child's time at Armani).
5. A "Ready for School" readiness summary — areas of strength and any areas the receiving school should know about.

> The Graduation Portfolio is presented to the family at a brief **Graduation Ceremony** (15–20 minutes, held in the center) during the child's final week.

### Handoff to Receiving School
If the parent requests it (or if the child has a documented developmental need):
- Armani prepares a formal **School Readiness Report** addressed to the receiving school's headteacher.
- This is sealed, handed to the parent, and copied to the child's digital record.

---

## 3. Alumni List & Referral Pipeline

When a child graduates, they are moved to **Alumni status** in the OS. The following actions are triggered:

1. The child's profile is archived (not deleted) — data retained for 5 years per `data-privacy.md`.
2. The family is added to the **Armani Alumni Circle** — a light-touch communication list:
   - Annual "How is [child's name] doing?" message at the start of each school year.
   - Invitation to Armani open days for siblings or referred families.
3. If a graduating family refers a new enrollment within 12 months, they receive the **Alumni Referral Reward** (defined in `growth-engine.md`).

---

## 4. Early Departures (Non-Graduation Exits)

If a family leaves before graduation (relocation, financial, dissatisfaction):
1. Branch Manager conducts a **15-minute exit conversation** (in person or phone).
2. Reason is logged (confidentially) in the child's record — feeds the quality scorecard.
3. Family still receives a **Mini Portfolio** (milestone summary + 2 photos) as a goodwill gesture.
4. Alumni list entry is created. The door is left open.
