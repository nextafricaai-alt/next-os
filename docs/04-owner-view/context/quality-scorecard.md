> The single number that tells Hudson whether a center is good — and whether the company is ready to open the next one. Score each component 0–100 from its source data; weightings below.

## Bands
- 🟢 85+ — expansion-grade
- 🟡 70–84 — solid, fix ambers
- 🔴 <70 — owner attention this week

## Rule
A new center may not open until the existing center(s) score 🟢 two consecutive months. Quality is the growth engine, not the brake.
