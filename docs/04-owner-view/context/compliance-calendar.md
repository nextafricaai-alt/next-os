# Compliance & Licensing Calendar

> Protecting the legal and operational status of Armani OS centers. A missed renewal is an existential threat to the business.

## Rules & Alert Thresholds
1. **The 60/30/15 Rule:** 
   - 60 days out: Initial alert to Branch Manager to begin the renewal process.
   - 30 days out: Escalation alert to the Global Director.
   - 15 days out: Critical red alert. The Director must take over the process manually.

## Core Required Documents
### 1. Facility & Operations
- **Ministry of Education / Local Council License:** Validates the center as an educational/childcare facility. (Renews Annually).
- **Fire Safety Certificate:** From the local fire department. Requires an annual inspection of extinguishers and alarms. (Renews Annually).
- **Health Inspector Certificate:** Clears the kitchen, washrooms, and food handling processes. (Renews Annually).
- **Liability Insurance Policy:** Covers accidents on premises. (Renews Annually).

### 2. Staff Certifications
- **First Aid & CPR Certification:** Required for *every* staff member on the floor. (Renews every 2 Years).
- **Food Handling Certificate:** Required for all kitchen staff and educators handling snacks. (Renews Annually).
- **Early Childhood Education (ECE) License:** Required for Lead Teachers. (Renews every 3 Years).

## The Weekly Routine
- The Branch Manager must review the Compliance Tracker in the OS every Monday.
- Any document entering the 60-day window must have a renewal application filed by Friday of that week.
