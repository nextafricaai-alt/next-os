# Forward Risk Register & Insurance Mapping

> Reactive incident logging is not enough. A mature childcare network anticipates risks before they occur and maps them to operational mitigations and insurance policies.

## 1. Physical & Safety Risks
- **Choking/Allergies:** Mitigated by the color-coded placemat system and strict kitchen controls. (Covered by General Liability Insurance).
- **Wandering/Elopement:** Mitigated by the digital sign-in/out gatekeeper system and visitor escort rule.
- **Fire/Evacuation:** Mitigated by monthly fire drills logged in the compliance calendar.

## 2. Financial & Reputational Risks
- **Cash Leakage:** Mitigated by the digital billing engine and strict petty cash controls. (Covered by Employee Dishonesty Insurance).
- **Social Media Crisis:** A child injury or staff misconduct going viral. Mitigated by the 1-hour Crisis Communication playbook.
- **Licensing Lapses:** Mitigated by the 60-day alerts in the Compliance Calendar.

## 3. Quarterly Risk Audit
The Global Director must review this register every quarter, ensuring that all insurance policies are active, coverage limits are sufficient, and operational mitigations are actually being practiced by the branches.
