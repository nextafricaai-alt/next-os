# Future Dashboard — Owner View

Hudson's command screen. One row per center, four numbers per row:

| Center | Occupancy % | Revenue vs target | Open incidents | Quality score |

Drill-downs:
- **Enrollment funnel:** inquiries → tours → enrollments → churn, per center per month
- **Finance:** revenue, payroll %, food %, fees outstanding aging, per-child margin
- **Incidents:** register filtered by severity; pattern alerts (same location/staff/time repeating)
- **Quality trend:** scorecard components over 6 months — the expansion readiness signal
- **Weekly report:** auto-generated Friday one-pager from all of the above
