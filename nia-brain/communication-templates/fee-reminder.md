# Fee Reminder Templates

Use these as the starting structure when drafting fee reminders for
Peak Primary (or any school tenant). Always adapt — never send the
template raw.

## The structure (every fee reminder)

1. **Warm opening using the guardian's name** — Mrs. / Mr. + surname.
   Never just "Dear parent."
2. **Acknowledgment of the relationship** — "Thank you for your
   continued partnership with Peak Primary."
3. **The factual note** — the balance and term. No drama, no urgency
   theatre.
4. **An offering, not a demand** — installment options, contact for
   discussion, payment channels.
5. **Warm close** — signature, school name, optional Luganda phrase.

## Three tone levels

### Level 1 — First reminder (just past due, gentle)

> Dear Mrs. Asiimwe,
>
> Thank you for your continued partnership with Peak Primary School.
> This is a gentle reminder that Ruth's Term 2 fees of UGX 180,000
> remain outstanding.
>
> If you would like to spread this across two installments, we are
> happy to arrange. You can reach our bursar on +256... or reply to
> this message.
>
> Webale nnyo for your understanding.
>
> Peak Primary School Office

### Level 2 — Second reminder (15-30 days overdue, firmer)

> Dear Mrs. Asiimwe,
>
> We hope Ruth is doing well at home. We're writing to follow up on
> Term 2 fees of UGX 180,000, now outstanding for [X] weeks.
>
> We understand circumstances vary — please reach out so we can find
> a workable arrangement together. We would much rather hear from you
> than chase a number.
>
> The bursar is available on +256... weekdays 8am–4pm.
>
> With warm regards,
> Peak Primary School Office

### Level 3 — Third reminder (30+ days, serious but still warm)

> Dear Mrs. Asiimwe,
>
> We are writing because Ruth's Term 2 fees (UGX 180,000) have now
> been outstanding for over 30 days, and we have not yet been able to
> reach you.
>
> Ruth is a valued part of our P3 Prudent stream, and we want to
> continue supporting her education. Could we ask you to call the
> bursar on +256... by Friday so we can discuss a way forward?
>
> If there are circumstances we should know about, please do tell us.
> We are here to find a solution with you.
>
> With concern and respect,
> [Head Teacher name]

## What to never write

- "Failure to pay will result in..." (cold, threatening)
- "Your child will be sent home if..." (humiliating the child)
- "URGENT" or excessive capitals (panicky, undignified)
- Long paragraphs justifying the school's costs (defensive)

## What Hudson would always do

- Use first names when possible
- Frame the school as a partner in the child's education, not a
  service provider chasing payment
- Offer a real path forward (installments, conversation, contact)
- Sign off with warmth — Luganda phrase if the recipient speaks it
