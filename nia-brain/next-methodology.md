# NEXT Methodology

NEXT is an African AI transformation company. The thesis: Africa
shouldn't fall behind in the intelligent age. NEXT helps organizations
transform — schools, hospitals, NGOs, churches, companies, governments —
by building them custom operating systems powered by AI.

## The mothership-fleet model

NEXT is the mothership. Each client gets a vertical OS — a Schools OS,
a Hospitals OS, an NGOs OS, a Church OS. The mothership supervises the
fleet through Sentinel (the agent layer, public face: Nia).

```
NEXT (mothership)
 ├── Sentinel (Nia) — supervises all client OSes
 ├── Schools OS  (Peak Primary School — live)
 ├── Hospitals OS (planned)
 ├── NGOs OS     (planned)
 ├── Churches OS (planned)
 ├── Homes OS    (planned)
 └── Companies OS (planned)
```

Every client OS shares the same spine (auth, finance, comms, AI tools)
but has vertical-specific surfaces (a school sees students and
attendance; an NGO sees beneficiaries and reports).

## The three tiers

NEXT clients buy one of three tiers:

1. **Catalyst — $149 / month**
   - For small organizations starting their digital journey
   - Includes: core OS access, 3 AI tools, basic dashboards, monthly
     check-ins
   - Best for: small schools, single-location NGOs, growing churches

2. **Builder — $749 / month**
   - For established organizations ready to scale
   - Includes: full vertical OS, custom integrations, advanced
     dashboards, weekly Nia supervision, team training
   - Best for: mid-size schools (200+ students), multi-program NGOs,
     larger churches

3. **Architect — Custom**
   - For organizations operating at scale or with complex needs
   - Includes: custom-built OS, dedicated NEXT engineer, advanced
     fine-tuning, board-level reporting, white-label options
   - Best for: networks (e.g. a foundation running 12 schools),
     government departments, large NGOs

## What NEXT is NOT

- NEXT is not a generic software vendor. We don't sell licenses; we
  build transformation.
- NEXT is not a Western tech transplant. We're built for African
  context — WhatsApp dominance, M-Pesa, multi-lingual (English,
  Luganda, Swahili, Kinyarwanda), intermittent connectivity.
- NEXT is not a development agency. We don't take "build me a
  website" briefs. We take "transform my organization" briefs.
