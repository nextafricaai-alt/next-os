# Charis Creations Playbook

Charis Creations is Hudson's media production and communications
company. It runs alongside NEXT — Charis tells the stories, NEXT
builds the systems.

## What Charis does

- Photography (events, portraits, lifestyle, documentary)
- Videography (events, brand films, documentary, podcast production)
- Digital storytelling (long-form content, social campaigns)
- Media strategy (positioning, comms calendars, content systems)
- Brand consulting (for mission-driven orgs)

## Who Charis serves

- Schools and educational institutions
- Churches and faith-based organizations
- NGOs and development programs
- Mission-driven brands
- Individuals — leaders, pastors, authors who need a media presence

The thread: organizations and individuals who have a real story but
don't have the media muscle to tell it well.

## Notable engagements

- **Fathers Arise** (under The Remnant Generation) — Hudson supports

  fatherhood movement.

## Charis pricing approach

Charis doesn't publish rates publicly. Every engagement gets a custom
proposal because the deliverables vary wildly (one shoot vs. a
six-month brand build).

The three engagement shapes:

1. **Project work** — discrete deliverable (a film, an event shoot, a
   campaign). Quoted as a fixed fee.
2. **Retainer** — ongoing media support, usually monthly. Best for
   organizations with constant content needs (a growing church, an
   NGO with regular reports).
3. **Strategic consulting** — Hudson personally advising on
   storytelling, positioning, comms architecture. Higher-touch, higher
   fee.

## Charis vs NEXT — when which one fits

| Client need | Charis | NEXT |
|---|---|---|
| "We need a brand film" | YES | no |
| "We need a parent-facing portal" | no | YES |
| "We need our story told better" | YES | no |
| "We need our operations digitized" | no | YES |
| "We need both — story AND systems" | both | both |

Many of Charis's strongest clients become NEXT clients because once
the story is clear, the systems gap becomes obvious.

## Hudson's Charis voice

Charis content is warm, cinematic, story-first. It avoids the
slick-but-soulless aesthetic of generic agency work. Charis would
rather a piece feel honest than perfect.
