📱 Tell Charis I'm Ready
📍 The Space
✓
Bride's prep room is tidy, clear, and clutter-free
↓ Why this matters
When your room tells a beautiful story, every corner becomes a backdrop. We are shooting your morning constantly — the windowsill, the mirror, the moment your mother does up your zip. A clear space lets us focus entirely on you, not on clearing away last night's bags or moving chairs out of frame.
✓
Groom's room is organised and presentable
↓ Why this matters
The groom's prep is often the most natural and emotionally raw footage we capture — the laughter, the nerves, the tie that won't cooperate. We want nothing in the background competing with that. A clean room puts the men front and centre, exactly where they should be.
💍 Your Details
✓
Dress, rings, shoes, veil, and bouquet are all in one spot
↓ Why this matters
The detail shoot is one of the most important parts of your album — and most people never realise it until they see the final photos. When everything is gathered together, we can arrange and photograph each item deliberately, artistically, as part of your story. When items are scattered across three rooms, we spend 30 minutes gathering instead of shooting. That time cannot be recovered.
✓
A beautiful wooden or custom hanger is ready for the dress
↓ Why this matters
The dress-on-hanger photograph is one of the most requested and most shared images from any wedding. A wooden or personalised hanger transforms that shot into something frame-worthy. A white plastic boutique hanger will appear in your photos for the rest of your life. This costs almost nothing to prepare and makes an enormous difference to the image.
✓
All jewellery and accessories are laid out and waiting
↓ Why this matters
Rings, earrings, bracelet, hair piece, perfume — each of these gets its own moment in front of the lens. They are the quiet details that tell the story of who you are on this day. Have them waiting, together, somewhere with good light.
💄 Hair & Makeup
✓
Bride is scheduled to be the FIRST person in the chair — not the last
↓ Why this matters
This is the single most important timing decision of your morning. If your bridesmaids go first and run long, your time in the chair shrinks. Every minute of delay at makeup is a photography moment that disappears from your album. You only get one morning as a bride. Protect it. Your bridesmaids will understand — they love you.
✓
Bride will be fully ready at least 45 minutes before departure
↓ Why this matters
Those last 45 minutes — fully dressed, fully done — are where some of the most powerful photographs happen. The quiet moment in front of the mirror. Your mother seeing you. Your bridesmaids gathered around you. These moments cannot be posed or recreated. They happen once, in real time, and only if you have given yourself the time to breathe.
✓
Your hair and makeup artist knows our crew will be shooting during the process
↓ Why this matters
Getting-ready photographs are some of the most intimate in your entire album. When the MUA knows we are present, they naturally position themselves and work in a way that keeps you in the frame beautifully. A good MUA is part of your story — let them know we will be capturing it.
👥 The People Around You
✓
Only bridesmaids, immediate family, and vendors are in the prep room
↓ Why this matters
More people means more energy, more noise, more movement — and more faces appearing in your photographs that are not part of your story. Every extra person in the room adds stress to a morning that should feel calm and intimate. Everyone else loves you and can celebrate with you at the reception. Give yourself this morning.
✓
Parents and close siblings are nearby during the getting-ready moments
↓ Why this matters
The moment a mother sees her daughter in her dress for the first time. The moment a father straightens his son's tie in the mirror and neither of them says a word. These are the photographs people frame and keep for the rest of their lives. We cannot stage them. We can only be there when they happen — and only if your family is present.
⏱️ Give Us Time
✓
Our crew has at least 1.5 hours at the bride's prep location
↓ Why this matters
This is not about us — it is about your album. Thirty minutes of prep coverage gives you approximately 30 usable photographs of getting ready. Ninety minutes gives you well over 200. That difference appears in your album on every page. Every. Single. Page.
✓
Groom's location and departure time is confirmed and shared with Charis
↓ Why this matters
Our crew splits between locations on your morning. Knowing exactly where the groom will be and when he is leaving means we can be there for his moments too — not rushing in at the last minute, but already in position, already part of the morning.
🕐 On the Day
✓
Your day-of coordinator or contact person has our crew's number
↓ Why this matters
Weddings change in real time — a gate that's locked, a ceremony that starts early, a programme that shifts. When someone on the ground can reach us directly, we adapt instantly. When they can't, we miss moments trying to find out what's happening.
✓
Any planned surprises (performances, entrances, reveals) have been noted in our intake form
↓ Why this matters
A surprise we know about is a moment we can capture beautifully. A surprise we don't know about is a moment we might miss entirely. Tell us everything — we are professional secret-keepers. Not knowing costs you the shot.
