The Ideal Wedding Day Timeline
The difference between a good wedding album and a great one is often not the photographer — it is the time they were given. This is how we recommend your day flows.
The truth about wedding photography: We cannot manufacture time. If your ceremony starts late, we cannot go back and capture the details we missed. If the photo session is cut short, those are images that simply do not exist. This timeline is not about us — it is about protecting the moments that matter most to you.

Phase 1 — Morning Preparation
📸
T minus 3 hours
Charis crew arrives — detail shoot begins
Dress, rings, shoes, bouquet, jewellery — all photographed while hair and makeup begins. This is the foundation of your album's opening pages.
💄
T minus 2.5 hours
Bride in the chair — getting-ready coverage begins
We move between bride, room, and details continuously. The story of your morning is being told in real time.
👗
T minus 1 hour
Bride fully done — getting dressed moment
This is one of the most important moments of the morning. Fully done, then dressed. Never the other way around.
✨
T minus 45 minutes
Quiet time — family moments, portraits, mirror shots
This is when the most powerful photographs happen. The room is calm. You are ready. Your mother sees you. Your father sees you. Nothing can be rushed here — it must be lived.
🚗
T minus 30 minutes
Departure for ceremony venue
Final checks, bouquet in hand, car door open. We capture the departure — one of the most cinematic moments of the day.
⚠️ The Makeup Delay Problem
The single most common cause of a compressed morning is hair and makeup running late. When this happens, the detail shoot gets shortened, the getting-dressed moment gets rushed, and the quiet family time disappears entirely. Bride goes first. Always.
Phase 2 — The Ceremony
⛪
Ceremony begins
Full ceremony coverage
We position based on the venue's restrictions. If there are areas we cannot access, we adapt — always finding the best available position for the moment.
💒
Ceremony ends
Exit, confetti, first moments as married couple
This moment is gold. Do not rush through it. Walk slowly. Hold each other. Let the people celebrate around you. We are capturing all of it.
Phase 3 — The Photo Session
📷
Ceremony + 15 minutes
Group and family photos begin
We work through your group shot list systematically. The more organised your family groupings are, the faster and better this goes. Have someone who can call people — it saves 20 minutes.
💑
Ceremony + 45 minutes
Couple's session — cinematic portraits
Just the two of you. This is where we create the images that go on your wall. Give us at least 30 minutes here — ideally 45. The golden light does not wait.
🎬
Last 10 minutes of photo session
Cinema crew's creative time
We will ask for 10 minutes to work with you for the cinematic film sequences — slow motion, artistic angles, the moments that make your highlight film extraordinary.
🚘
Photo session ends
Head to reception — coverage continues en route
The drive, the arrival, the entrance — all part of the story.
Phase 4 — The Reception
🥂
Reception begins
Entrances, décor, atmosphere coverage
We arrive before you to photograph the space — the tables, the flowers, the light — before the guests fill it. Then we capture your entrance.
💃
Key moments — tell us the times
First dance · Speeches · Cake cutting · Bouquet toss · Send-off
We need to know what time these happen. Not an estimate — a time. When we know, we are positioned and ready before the moment begins. When we don't, we may be reloading equipment on the other side of the room.
🍽️
Meal service
Crew meal break — served same time as guests
Our crew does not photograph guests eating — it is not dignified. Meal service is our reset time. We return fully ready for the rest of the evening. Please ensure our meal is served at the same time as guests, not after.
🌙
Coverage end time
Final coverage — send-off or exit shot
The last frame of your day. Make it count — plan an exit that gives us something beautiful to end on.
